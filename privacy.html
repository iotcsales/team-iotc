Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This policy covers the LandOS and FasOS tools built and distributed by Team IOTC (Indus Overseas Trading Corporation), including Verdeed, Dealtally, Brokertally, Veritally, Clearsum, Truestay, Landtally, and Margintally.

No accounts and browser-based storage

None of our tools currently require you to create an account or sign in. Most calculations and processing take place directly in your browser.

Where a tool offers a "History" feature, that history is stored only in your browser's local storage and is not transmitted to or stored on Team IOTC servers. Clearing your browser data may permanently erase locally stored history.

Verdeed is different: when you use its AI analysis features, information you submit is transmitted directly to Anthropic's API for processing, as described below.

AI features (Verdeed)

Verdeed uses your own Anthropic API key (bring-your-own-key). Your API key is handled by the Verdeed application in your browser and is sent directly to Anthropic's API when you request AI processing. Team IOTC does not receive or store your Anthropic API key on its servers.

Documents, text, and other information you submit to Verdeed for AI analysis are transmitted directly to Anthropic for processing. Such processing is subject to Anthropic's applicable privacy policy and terms. Team IOTC does not control Anthropic's processing of information once it has been transmitted to Anthropic.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights and permissions to submit documents and other information for processing.

Payments

Paid tools are sold through Gumroad. We do not collect or store your payment card details. Gumroad processes transactions and handles payment-related information under its own privacy policy.

We may receive your name, email address, purchase information, and transaction-related information from Gumroad as necessary to fulfill your purchase, provide customer support, maintain appropriate business records, and address refunds or disputes.

Cookies and tracking

Our website does not currently use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services.

Some essential browser or technical storage may be used where necessary for the operation, security, or functionality of the website and tools.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, provide appropriate notice or obtain consent.

Data we do collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive:

  • Your name and email address when you contact us for support.
  • Purchase and transaction information provided by Gumroad in connection with your purchase.
  • Information you voluntarily provide in communications with us.
  • Other information reasonably necessary to provide customer support, process requests, maintain business records, or address disputes.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

How we use information

We may use information we receive to:

  • Fulfill and support purchases.
  • Respond to customer-support requests.
  • Communicate with customers about purchases, access, refunds, or support matters.
  • Maintain business and transaction records.
  • Protect the security and integrity of our tools and services.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Detect, prevent, or address fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, or other misuse.

Your documents and content

Information that you submit to Verdeed for AI analysis is transmitted directly to Anthropic for processing as described in the "AI Features (Verdeed)" section.

Team IOTC does not intentionally collect or store Verdeed documents on its own servers where the application architecture operates as described above.

You remain responsible for ensuring that you have the legal right and necessary permission to submit any documents, data, text, images, or other materials for processing.

Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, fulfill purchases, provide customer support, maintain appropriate business and transaction records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and protect our legitimate business interests.

Where information is no longer required for these purposes, we may delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Third-party services

We use certain third-party services to operate our business and provide our tools, including Gumroad for payment and digital-product transactions and Anthropic for AI processing in Verdeed.

These third parties operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage you to review their applicable policies before using the relevant services.

International processing

Because we use third-party service providers that may operate in different countries, information processed through those services may be handled outside India.

Where required, such processing will be subject to applicable legal requirements and the relevant provider's privacy policies.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your privacy rights and privacy requests

Subject to applicable law and the timing of any applicable legal requirements, you may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to raise a privacy-related concern.
