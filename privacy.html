Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This policy covers the LandOS and FasOS tools built and distributed by Team IOTC (Indus Overseas Trading Corporation), including Verdeed, Dealtally, Brokertally, Veritally, Clearsum, Truestay, Landtally, and Margintally.

No accounts, no server-side storage

None of our tools require you to create an account or sign in. Calculations and analysis run directly in your browser. Where a tool offers a "History" feature, that history is saved only in your own browser's local storage — it is never transmitted to us or stored on any server. Clearing your browser data will permanently erase it.

AI features (Verdeed)

Verdeed uses your own Anthropic API key (bring-your-own-key). Your key is stored only in your browser for the duration of your session and is sent directly to Anthropic's API to process your request — it never passes through or is stored on any Team IOTC server. Document text and files you submit for analysis are sent directly to Anthropic for processing and are subject to Anthropic's own privacy policy, not ours.

Payments

Paid tools are sold through Gumroad. We do not collect or store your payment card details — Gumroad processes all transactions and is governed by Gumroad's privacy policy. We may receive your name and email from Gumroad to fulfill your purchase and provide support.
